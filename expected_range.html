The calculation
Range = Strike × IV/100 × √(days / 365)

One formula, three clocks. DTE uses whole days. Intraday counts from the snapshot time to expiry. Realtime counts from now to expiry and re-prices every second. Once an expiry has passed, that instrument rolls forward one day, exactly as the source spreadsheet does. Levels are the strike plus and minus half the selected range.

Option expiry times

    Treasuries settle at 17:00 ET on Fridays and 15:00 ET on every other day; the rule is applied to the date of the expiry being counted toward. Expiries are held in exchange time, so they stay correct through both the US and the European daylight-saving switches.
